FAQs

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Talk to us directly.

Book a 30-minute meeting
What kind of companies do you work with?

Two groups. Impact-driven startups and organisations building from zero to one, including enterprises launching new flagship products. And venture capital and private equity firms, for their in-house tools and for portfolio companies that need structure. We are industry agnostic, with deep experience in data-heavy products, platforms and software.

How do engagements work?

We work in three ways: fractional, advisory and retainer. Fractional puts senior product design and leadership inside your team part-time. Advisory is focused guidance at key decisions. Retainer is ongoing design and product support. We’ll recommend the right fit on a first call.

What does a Founding MVP engagement include?

Zero-to-one product design, from first concept to a launch-ready first version: research, UX, UI and a design system your engineers can build from. The goal is a product people actually want to use, and one you can sell.

Can you help with a product that’s already live?

Yes. We run UI/UX overhauls and optimisation for products already in market, and conversion-focused (CRO) design for e-commerce stores. For Surreal, revenue per user rose 150% within a week of launch.

Do you design AI and agentic products?

Yes. We design agentic workflows and AI-native flows around how people actually work, so automation earns trust instead of adding friction.

We’re a VC or PE firm. How do you support our portfolio?

We design in-house tools for your firm and step into portfolio companies working through ambiguity and chaos, bringing structure, clarity and hands-on product consultation so teams start shipping again.

Do you only do design, or product management and leadership too?

Both. Alongside full-stack product design, we offer product management (roadmapping, prioritisation and discovery) and interim or fractional design and product leadership.

How do we get started?

Book a 30-minute meeting on Calendly, or message the founder directly on WhatsApp. We’ll talk through where you are and what would help most.
