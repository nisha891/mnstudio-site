AI-assisted development

The gap between
the deck and the build
is where products die

Traditional design hands over a file and hopes. We use AI-assisted development to close that gap ourselves — turning flows and decisions into working, reviewable software inside the same week they're made.

The point isn't speed for its own sake. It's that a real prototype settles arguments a static mockup can only start, and your engineers inherit something concrete instead of an interpretation.

Talk through a build →︎
  1. Step 01

    Decide

    A flow, a screen or a mechanic is agreed in the room — with the tradeoffs written down.

  2. Step 02

    Generate

    AI tooling drafts the interface and the scaffolding; we direct, edit and hold the quality line.

  3. Step 03

    Test

    Real users and your own team click something real — not a prototype held together by hotspots.

  4. Step 04

    Hand over

    Documented components, tokens and code your engineers can adopt, extend or throw away knowingly.

How we work

Short loops,
written decisions

  1. 01

    Frame

    A week of listening — users, team, data and constraints — ending in a written problem statement we both sign.

  2. 02

    Focus

    Options on the table, tradeoffs made explicit, one direction chosen. Scope is cut here, not later.

  3. 03

    Build

    Design and AI-assisted implementation run together, reviewed in the open every few days.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Systems, documentation and coaching so the standard holds after we step out.
