Reunion PWA: audit-ready compliance for clean energy tax credits.

We designed Reunion PWA from zero to one: the easiest way for clean energy projects to prove Prevailing Wage and Apprenticeship compliance and secure the full value of their tax credits. It’s built for C-level executives at Fortune 500 and publicly traded companies in the US, and for the contractors who keep their projects compliant.

The problem

Compliance that matters, done by hand.

Clean energy projects over 1MW that began construction after January 29, 2023 must meet Prevailing Wage and Apprenticeship (PWA) requirements to secure 100% of their tax credit value. Get it wrong and a project can lose up to 80% of its tax credits, along with its reputation.

Proving compliance meant tracking payroll across every contractor and subcontractor, verifying apprenticeship ratios every day and staying audit-ready. It was done manually or with costly third-party consultants, and buyers on the other side of the deal demand rigorous due diligence.

Reunion PWA had to make that easy:

01

Real-time tracking

Wage, fringe and apprenticeship requirements tracked from contractors’ payroll reports.

02

Automatic checks

Wages compared against up-to-date Department of Labor wage determinations.

03

Penalties & cures

Penalties calculated automatically, with cure payments tracked and documented.

04

Shareable proof

An on-demand final report for tax credit buyers and insurers, to speed up due diligence.

Who we designed for

From the boardroom to the job site.

C-level executives

Leaders at Fortune 500 and publicly traded US companies buying or selling tax credits. They need to see at a glance whether a portfolio is compliant, and trust the answer enough to sign off on a deal.

Developers, contractors & subcontractors

The teams uploading payroll reports, adding employees and running apprentice programs. They need a user-friendly way to get the details right, without being compliance experts.

  1. Step 1Upload project details and payroll reports
  2. Step 2Track wages and apprentice requirements in real time
  3. Step 3Generate reports on demand
  4. Step 4Secure full tax credit value without guesswork

The product

Every requirement, one source of truth.

Accessibility

Clear for everyone, from the CFO to the crew.

Compliance data carries real money and legal risk, and it’s read by executives on a laptop between meetings as much as by contractors on site. Accessibility was a requirement from the first wireframe, not a final check.

Status in words, not just colour

Every status is written out: Compliant, Not compliant, Good Faith, Exempt, Missing. People with colour blindness, or anyone printing a report, never has to rely on red and green.

High contrast, readable data

Dark text on light backgrounds, a restrained palette and clear column headers keep dense compliance tables easy to scan and meet contrast guidelines.

Forms that explain themselves

Every field has a visible label, required fields are marked, and plain-language helper text explains terms like journeyworker ratio or contract execution date.

Plain language for the boardroom

Regulatory detail is turned into answers executives can act on: is this project compliant, what’s at risk, and what does it cost to fix.

Keyboard-friendly workflows

Forms, tables and dialogs follow a logical order with visible focus, so heavy data entry like payroll reports works without a mouse.

Consistent, predictable layout

The same navigation, page structure and actions on every screen, so the product is easy to learn for occasional users and those using assistive technology.
